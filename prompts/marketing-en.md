The video follows the customer's journey from the first time they see your content to the moment they book with you. Then it reveals how the product solves the problems on that journey, and it ends on one simple call to action (for example, book a 15-minute call).
The animations serve the words: show the problem visually, then make the solution clear, and in the data part turn numbers and visits into something the viewer can picture using in their own work. Not slides full of text: the brand's real page is part of the story.

Style: modern, clean, premium motion graphics with shots of the real page. About 2:30–3:00 depending on the voice's pace.

Scene 1 — The problem: the customer's journey (00:00–00:25)
Voice: Have you ever thought about what someone goes through, from seeing your content to deciding to work with you? First they see one of your videos and get interested. Then they want to know more, so they watch your other videos and posts and check the comments for what other people say about you.
Animation: a phone showing a creator's video with a gentle scroll; a like or a comment appears, then a tap on the profile; a quick trip through videos, posts and comments; the pieces pile up in front of the viewer to show how much they have to dig through.

Scene 2 — The turning point (00:25–00:50)
Voice: the same questions every day (how much is it? when are you free? how do I book?), late replies, content scattered across platforms. Then, calmly: "The problem isn't your content. It's what happens after someone gets interested in you."
Animation: the questions appear as message bubbles; then the turning-point sentence fades in alone, with a short pause in the voice.

Scene 3 — A page made for you (00:50–01:25)
Voice: we build you a personal page made for you and your kind of work. Not a template with your name swapped in: it is designed around who you are, what you offer and what you want people to understand. Colours, layout, how services are shown, even the small animations reflect your identity. And the page brings everything about you into one place: your story, services, work, client reviews.
Animation: a blank page builds itself in front of the viewer, colours, type, pictures and sections arriving; 2–3 parts of the real design, moving smoothly between the phone and desktop versions; zoom on services, work and reviews. On "not a template", flash a generic template, then replace it with the custom design.

Scene 4 — Bookings and calendars (01:25–01:45)
Voice: and when someone decides to book? They see your free times, pick one, and the booking lands in your calendar. If you use more than one calendar, they stay in sync, so a slot taken in one never shows as free in the other.
Animation: a tap on "Book with me"; free slots appear; a slot is picked and turns into a confirmed booking; two calendars side by side, the booking appears in the first and the second updates, with a sync mark between them; the taken slot disappears from the other calendar.

Scene 5 — From guessing to understanding the data (01:45–02:25)
Voice: how do you know which content actually brings results? With a different link for each post, you see where visitors come from, which country, which post or channel, and what they looked at on your page. That tells you what works, what to change, and what deserves more focus, even which ideas to test in ads, instead of guessing.
Animation: three posts (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), each with its own link, sending visitors to the page; a dashboard with traffic sources, countries, most-viewed sections and time spent; the best post highlighted, then a new ad idea built from it. On screen at the end: "Stop guessing. Let the data pick your next step."
Dashboard numbers must be real or clearly marked as examples.

Scene 6 — More than a page, and the call to action (02:25–02:55)
Voice: it's not just a page we hand over. We want to help you understand your business, see what brings results and take your next steps on clear data. If you'd like to know how it can help your work, book a 15-minute call from the link below. See you on the call!
Animation: back to the page, then a quick closing montage of bookings and the dashboard; the stages "Your page → Bookings → Insights" linked together; a big "Book a 15-minute call" button with an arrow pointing at the real link.

Production notes:
- Voice: natural and calm, like explaining the idea to a business owner, not reading an ad.
- Pace: quicker through the problems, calmer at the reveal, clear in the data part and the call to action.
- Transitions: smooth motion graphics; avoid effects that distract from the message.
- On-screen text: a few short words only ("Your own page", "Bookings sorted", "Know your audience"), never the whole script.
- Honesty: use the real product whenever possible; never show features or data as real unless the product really supports them.
The main point: sell the result, not a feature list. The client understands you easily, books with you easily, and you understand what your content does.
